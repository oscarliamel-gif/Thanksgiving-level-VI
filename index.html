🦃 Thanksgiving Escape Room

Grammar: Passive Voice · Time: 30 minutes

⏱ Time left:30:00 (starts when you type your name)

📜 Instructions

  1. The Thanksgiving dinner is locked! You are in a house with 10 rooms. You have 30 minutes to open all the doors. The timer starts when you type your name.
  2. Type your name above to open Room 1. The other rooms are locked: a room opens only when the room before it is solved.
  3. Do all the activities in the room. Then press Check my answers.
  4. If everything is correct, the door opens and you get a secret letter. If not, you must wait and try again. Read carefully!
  5. Write each letter in the Code Sheet at the end. Use the alphabet code to change each letter into a number.
  6. When all 10 rooms are solved, complete the Code Sheet and check it. Then the final code box unlocks. Enter your final code to open the dinner!
  7. Remember: passive voice = am/is/are/was/were + past participle. Example: The turkey was cooked by my father.

Room 1 · The First Thanksgiving

Reading · Multiple choice

The first Thanksgiving happened in 1621.
The Pilgrims lived in Plymouth.
The Wampanoag people helped them with food.

Today, a big turkey is cooked in many homes.
Families eat pumpkin pie for dessert.
Many people watch football after dinner.
Thanks are given for food, family, and friends.

1. Who helped the Pilgrims with food?
2. How long was the first celebration?
3. What do families eat for dessert?
